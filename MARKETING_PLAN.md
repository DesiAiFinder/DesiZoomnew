# DesiZoom — 90-day marketing plan

Businesses first, near-zero budget, 10–20 hrs/week, anchored on Diwali (~52 days out).

## The one-line strategy

Sign 15 paying-capable businesses in the Little Elm–Frisco–Plano corridor before
Diwali, and let each one bring you their customers. You are not marketing an app;
you are marketing *"your Diwali rush, without the 30% delivery-app tax."*

## Why businesses first works here

- Every business you sign has 500–5,000 existing customers. A restaurant that
  posts "order your Diwali sweets box on DesiZoom" to its own WhatsApp list is
  doing your user acquisition for you, free, with trust you can't buy.
- An empty deals page kills user retention; a full one *is* the product.
- Your pitch is genuinely strong: 6% per order vs ~30% on DoorDash/Uber Eats,
  free deal posts during launch, live order alerts, direct Stripe payouts,
  no tablet hardware.

## The merchant pitch (say it in this order)

1. "You're on DoorDash? They take about 30%. We take 6."
2. "Deals and your business page are free during launch."
3. "Orders ring on your phone like a text — you tap once to accept." (demo the
   live order bar on your phone, in front of them — this is your wow moment)
4. "Diwali is N weeks away. Families near you will be looking for sweets,
   catering, pooja items. Be listed before the rush."
5. Sign them up on the spot on THEIR phone — never "here's a link, do it later."

Close rate reality: expect 1 in 4. That's 60 conversations for 15 businesses.
At 10 walk-ins/week that's 6 weeks — exactly the runway you have.

## Target list (in priority order)

1. **Sweet shops & caterers** — Diwali is their Super Bowl; strongest urgency.
2. **Restaurants already doing pickup** — zero behavior change, instant fit.
3. **Priests & pooja services** — Diwali + wedding season; bookings engine is
   built; almost none of them have any online presence (easy to impress).
4. **Grocery stores** — high traffic; ask for a counter flyer even if they
   don't sign, because their foot traffic is your user base.
5. **Mehndi artists, decorators, photographers** — occasion-search categories
   are already in the app; wedding season follows Diwali.

Route: build the walk-in list from your own Deals page (the Google Places
"Near You" section is literally your prospect database, sorted by rating).

## Weekly cadence (fits in 10–20 hrs)

- **2 weekday evenings (2 hrs each):** walk into 5 businesses per evening,
  during their slow hours (2:30–5pm for restaurants).
- **Saturday morning:** temple + grocery store circuit. Flyers on noticeboards,
  talk to whoever manages events. Temples are also organizations you already
  seed in the app — tell them their events can sell tickets on DesiZoom (5%).
- **Sunday (1 hr):** post the week's new deals into 3–5 DFW desi Facebook/
  WhatsApp groups ("New this week on DesiZoom: X, Y, Z — all in Frisco").
  Share as a member, not an ad. One post per group per week, no more.
- **30 min/day:** respond to every merchant question instantly. At this stage
  YOU are the support team, and speed is the product.

## The user side (it rides on the business side)

- Every signed business gets a **QR table tent / counter card** ("Order pickup
  here — skip the apps, support us directly"). Their counter is your billboard.
- Every deal a business posts, you cross-post to community groups (above).
- **Diwali landing moment:** the week before Diwali, the homepage hero becomes
  a Diwali hub — sweets pre-orders, pooja bookings, garba/event tickets, deals.
  You already have the festival banner; make it a page worth sharing.
- Radio stations already in your app (FunAsia, Radio Sangam): ask about a
  community-calendar mention or interview. Local desi radio covers local desi
  startups — it's content for them. Free if you're interesting.

## What NOT to do (budget discipline)

- No paid ads until ~25 businesses are live. Ads into an empty marketplace
  burn money to show people a page that can't retain them.
- No expansion past the Frisco–Plano–Little Elm corridor until it's dense.
  Eight cities in the picker meant nothing; one full city means everything.
- No feature building as marketing procrastination. The app is ready enough.

## Numbers that tell you it's working

| Milestone            | Target      | By          |
|----------------------|-------------|-------------|
| Businesses signed    | 15          | Diwali      |
| Weekly walk-ins      | 10          | every week  |
| Orders/week          | 25          | Diwali week |
| Deals live at once   | 20          | Diwali week |
| Repeat-order rate    | 30%         | +30 days    |

Repeat-order rate is the one that matters most: it proves the loop works
without you pushing. Track it in the admin payments tab.

## After Diwali (weeks 8–12)

- Convert momentum into habit: "order Friday dinner" push via deal boosts.
- Wedding season begins — services side (priest, mehndi, venue, photo) takes
  over as the growth engine; same playbook, new category list.
- First paid experiment (if metrics above were hit): $200 into boosting the
  top 3 merchants' deals in local Facebook groups, measured against a week of
  organic baseline.
