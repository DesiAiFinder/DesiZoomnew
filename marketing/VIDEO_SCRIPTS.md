# Video scripts — record these on a phone

No studio needed. Phone screen recording plus your voice. Four videos, in the
order I'd make them.

## Setup (do this once)

- **Phone screen recording.** iPhone: Control Centre → Record. Android:
  Quick Settings → Screen record. Turn the microphone ON.
- **Portrait** for videos 1–3 (WhatsApp, Instagram, Status). **Landscape** for
  video 4 (website, YouTube).
- Do Not Disturb on. A notification banner mid-take ruins it.
- Have real data on screen before you start — a real restaurant, a real deal.
  Empty screens make it look dead.
- Speak slower than feels natural. Everyone rushes on the first take.
- Don't aim for perfect. Three takes maximum, pick the least bad.

# 1. Merchant demo — 90 seconds
**The most valuable one. This is what closes restaurant owners.**

Record with two phones: one showing the customer ordering, one showing your
business screen receiving it.

| Screen | Say |
|---|---|
| Deals page, their city | "This is DesiZoom — desi families in Little Elm and Frisco use it to find local restaurants and deals." |
| Tap a restaurant, menu opens | "A customer opens your menu…" |
| Add two items, tap Checkout | "…picks what they want, and pays." |
| **Cut to second phone: order alert appears, chime** | "And it lands on your phone straight away. That's it — that's the whole thing you have to do." |
| Tap "Start preparing" | "You tap once when you start cooking…" |
| Tap "Mark ready" | "…and once when it's ready. The customer sees it update live." |
| Cut back: customer's tracker moves to Ready | "They know exactly when to come and get it." |
| Payouts card on My Business | "Money goes straight to your bank through Stripe. We take 6%. No monthly fee, no tablet, no contract." |
| End on desizoom.com | "Send me a photo of your menu and I'll set the whole thing up for you." |

Send this on WhatsApp after every walk-in. It does the follow-up for you.

# 2. Customer quick tour — 60 seconds
**For your WhatsApp groups and Status.**

| Screen | Say |
|---|---|
| Home page, city picker showing their city | "DesiZoom finds everything desi near you — it picks up your city automatically." |
| Scroll: live now, in theatres, deals | "What's happening today, which Indian films are in theatres, deals near you." |
| Tap Deals | "Real deals from local restaurants and stores." |
| Tap Order Food, open a menu | "Order pickup straight from desi restaurants — no delivery app fees, no marked-up prices." |
| Tap Services | "Book a priest, a caterer, a mehndi artist." |
| Tap What's On / Events | "Garba nights, temple events, community programmes." |
| Home, tap radio | "And desi radio while you cook." |
| End on desizoom.com | "It's free, there's nothing to install. Little Elm, Frisco, Plano — desizoom.com." |

Keep it under a minute. On Status, most people drop off at forty seconds.

# 3. How to order — 30 seconds
**Give this to every merchant to send to their own customers.**

| Screen | Say |
|---|---|
| desizoom.com/order | "Ordering from [restaurant] is quick." |
| Tap the restaurant | "Open their menu…" |
| Add items | "…pick what you want…" |
| Checkout, pay | "…and pay. That's it." |
| Order tracker appears | "You'll see it update as they cook, and you'll know when it's ready to collect." |

No branding pitch. Just the mechanics. Merchants share this happily because
it helps their own customers.

# 4. Full walkthrough — 3 to 4 minutes
**For the website and for onboarding. Landscape.**

Sections, in this order. Keep each to about twenty seconds.

1. **Sign up** — `/register`, email and password, done. "Takes ten seconds."
2. **Your city** — city picker, GPS detection, radius selector. "It finds your
   city, or pick one."
3. **Home** — live now, movies in theatres, deals, radio, community news.
4. **Deals** — nearby businesses plus community-posted deals; explain the
   difference briefly.
5. **Order food** — restaurant list → menu → cart → checkout → live tracker.
   Spend the most time here; it's the core.
6. **Services** — occasion search. Type "wedding" and show the fan-out into
   venue, caterer, photographer, mehndi, priest. **This is your most original
   feature — don't rush it.**
7. **Events & tickets** — browse, buy a ticket, show it in the profile.
8. **Marketplace** — buying and selling, roommates and accommodation.
9. **Community** — Adda, news, live streams, local info.
10. **Post something** — the + Post button, choose a type, publish.
11. **For businesses** — Add Business wizard → My Business → live orders panel.
12. **Your profile** — listings, food orders, bookings, tickets, saved, money.

Close on: "Built in Little Elm, Texas. desizoom.com."
